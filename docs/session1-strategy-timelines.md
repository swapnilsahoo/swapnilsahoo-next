## Evidence decisions

- YouTube: Steve Chen's IEEE interview and the founders' Sequoia account support a deliberate opening after the dating proposition attracted no uploads. The MySpace channel is an emergent candidate, not proven organisational emergence. “Tune In, Hook Up” is described as a slogan. A zoo upload is not presented as the cause of the initial pivot.
- Starbucks: the official timeline and Il Giornale history distinguish the original bean-retail business, 1983 Italian experience, 1984 trial, 1985 Il Giornale founding and 1987 acquisition. Schultz's conscious café execution is deliberate relative to his own intention; a permanent documented beans-only intention is not asserted.
- Nano: Tata's price release qualifies ₹1 lakh as the Standard BS2 ex-factory Pantnagar price excluding transport/VAT. The annual report supports later GenX equipment; the May 2018 disclosure supplies that month's 11 production/20 domestic-sales figures. Weak demand is not itself emergent strategy. No single-cause failure account, lifetime sales total or causal claim about Tata's later company-wide strategy is made.
- Pfizer: a development history coauthored by Ian Osterloh, the published FDA approval record and Pfizer history support the initial cardiovascular programme, redirected research and 1998 Viagra launch. An unexpected biological effect is a learning signal; the later planned development is deliberate. The story does not offer treatment advice or claim sildenafil had no cardiovascular use.

The cases are original teaching interpretations of historical sources, not reproductions of company strategy plans or licensed course cases. External sources can change or restrict access independently.
