prototype URLs, and other personal information.

## Public-copy rule

Use the first three counts only with their stage labels:

- 29 raw registration records;
- 24 timestamped prototype-response rows;
- 10 teams announced for the final.

Do not present 26 as a unique-prototype count. If the 27 July summary is
mentioned, identify it as a later unreconciled record rather than silently
mixing its figures with the contemporaneous event files.

## Jury criteria

The 4 July final notice records a three-alumni jury and these five criteria:

1. clarity and relevance of the problem;
2. practicality of the GenAI-enabled solution;
3. quality and usability of the prototype or demonstration;
4. ease of adoption within Great Lakes Gurgaon;
5. potential institutional impact.

## Maintenance

Before changing a public count, preserve the source stage and date, document
the reconciliation method, and avoid exposing student-level records. If a
verified attendance or final score sheet later establishes why the final notice
and institutional summary differ, update this note and the public evidence note
together.
