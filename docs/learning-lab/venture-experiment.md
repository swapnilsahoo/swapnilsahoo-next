# Free 14-day student venture experiment

Implemented 21 October 2026. Public route: [/learning-lab/venture-experiment](https://www.swapnilsahoo.com/learning-lab/venture-experiment).

**Swapnil Sahoo Learning Lab helps first-time student entrepreneurs turn a business idea into a small, affordable customer experiment in 14 days, with practical guidance and feedback, free of charge.**

## Delivered product

The homepage and academic site's Lab card now lead with this offer. The KG-to-PhD ambition remains on the purpose page as the long-term direction; the proposed foundation and holding-company names remain proposed.

College students aged 18 or over can read all 14 daily tasks without signing up. Four stages produce an experiment boundary, an evidence log, test results and a supported continue/change/stop decision. The planner adjusts the next prepared task to the selected day, skills, relationships, available time, customer access and affordable commitment. It never spends money or contacts customers. Zero spending is valid; no access or time produces an honest feasibility task rather than an invented test. The original Asha example is explicitly fictional.

Browser drafts and worksheet downloads are optional. They contain learner-entered work, not an access code. No general newsletter subscription, payment, account password or certificate is required.

## Optional founder-feedback operation

Up to **20 active places**, enforced atomically in the existing database. Adult/student/privacy acknowledgements, input validation, same-origin checks, a honeypot and rate limits guard registration. A one-time random private code is shown after registration. Only its keyed hash is stored. The learner keeps the separate private-code download; it is not emailed, included in URLs or included in the public worksheet.

The learner submits each of four stages and returns with the code to read actual reviews. Prepared task guidance is not personal feedback. Dr. Swapnil Sahoo, through the existing authenticated permanent-admin page, can write feedback, score the four published criteria from 0–2 each and record actual review minutes. Review history retains the exact work assessed. Revision checks prevent stale learner edits or concurrent operator reviews from silently replacing newer versions. An identical retry does not double-count minutes. No instant feedback or fixed turnaround is promised.

The code expires 90 days after registration. Expired access is rejected. The next database maintenance or retention run removes expired venture records; exact-instant scheduled deletion is not claimed. Learner withdrawal deletes their pilot work and frees the place; the permanent operator can also delete it for a privacy request. The demo operator cannot read or review venture participants. New venture tables are included in encrypted logical backup and fresh-database restore.

No founder review has been fabricated. Launching the service does not deliver mentoring, recruit 20 participants or establish experiment completion. The older short-lesson pilot and its three sent invitations remain separate. There is no automatic invitation, reminder or research enrolment for this product.

## Evidence and comparison protocol

1. Before any comparison recruitment, define the learner criteria, allocation method, named alternative resource, tasks, time window, assessor procedure and consent. The product itself does not obtain research or publication consent. Keep service participation independent from grades, references and employment decisions.
2. Collect a dated starting plan **before exposure** to this guide or feedback if calling it an unaided baseline. If already exposed, label it aided; do not retroactively call it unaided. Retain the original plan. Score baseline and final work with the same four-criterion, 0–8 rubric and report assessor differences.
3. Record every starter, actual start/end dates, time spent, withdrawals, missing submissions and reasons when volunteered. A 14-day goal is not a measured 14-day completion. Do not drop non-completers from the denominator.
4. Human verification of completion requires an affordable boundary, dated customer evidence, a real offer/test with observed action **including refusals or zero uptake**, effort/cost records and a reasoned continue/change/stop decision. Four saved stages, a positive rating or a high rubric score alone do not verify that an actual test occurred. Do not request identifiable customer records, screenshots of private conversations or recordings as proof.
5. Compare comparable consenting learners using the alternative's normal free access with the same outcome definition, rubric and reporting window. Record differences in prior experience, constraints, access, attrition and mentoring. The initial 20-place service is not a controlled trial or a validated superiority result.
6. Sum actual recorded mentoring minutes across saved stage reviews; also log teaching, preparation, support and operational time outside reviews before estimating delivery cost. A recorded review count does not capture all founder labour.
7. Report completion, evidence quality and elapsed time separately from satisfaction. Publish sample sizes and missing outcomes. A stop decision can be a successful learning outcome; commercial success is neither required nor guaranteed. Do not infer causality or superiority from a small self-selected pilot. Obtain separate permission before publishing work, names, comments or comparative research.

Independent alternatives linked on the page are [YC Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/), the [Startup India Learning Program](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/santhali/content/sih/en/learning-and-development_v2.html) and [NSF I-Corps](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/i-corps). Their audiences and formats differ. No ranking, partnership or claim that the Lab is better is made.

## Validation

Isolated tests cover capacity races, per-learner isolation, hashed codes, expiry, withdrawal, role restrictions, learner/review revision conflicts, assessed-work snapshots, actual-minute retry handling and encrypted backup/fresh restore. Curriculum tests cover all 14 days, the four stages and the task planner's feasibility/budget boundaries. ESLint and TypeScript checks passed for the implementation. Production build and browser/HTTP release results are recorded after deployment; synthetic UI tests do not create participants or demonstrate learning.
