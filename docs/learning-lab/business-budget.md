## First AI cohort economics

Founder time is costed at a provisional **₹2,000/hour** opportunity cost. This is a planning valuation, not a salary commitment or assertion of market rate. At 12 learners: 9 teaching + 6 preparation + 8 assessment/support = **23 founder hours = ₹46,000**. Additional learner support scales at 2/3 hour per learner; capacity cannot expand without available feedback time.

| Item | Provisional assumption | At 12 learners and ₹8,000 fee |
|---|---|---:|
| Gross collected fees, excluding output GST | Learners × fee | ₹96,000 |
| Refund provision | 5% of gross collected fees; not a permitted-refund ceiling | ₹4,800 |
| Payment processing allowance | 3% of gross fees, including an allowance for provider taxes; not a quoted rate | ₹2,880 |
| Acquisition | ₹800 per enrolled learner, covering approved campaigns/outreach cash costs | ₹9,600 |
| Learner resources/support cash | ₹150 per learner | ₹1,800 |
| Software/database/hosting/video/email allocation | ₹3,500 per cohort; validate subscriptions, commercial-use eligibility and tax | ₹3,500 |
| Administration | 4 hours × ₹1,000, including receipts/reconciliation/coordination | ₹4,000 |
| Founder preparation/teaching | 15 fixed hours × ₹2,000 | ₹30,000 |
| Founder assessment/support | 2/3 hour × 12 × ₹2,000 | ₹16,000 |
| Contribution after delivery costs and founder time | Gross fees minus every row above | **₹23,420 (24.4%)** |

This margin excludes initial build/setup, general overhead, financing, income taxes and later company compliance. Founder teaching is not free because it is delivered by the founder. Refund provision must be replaced with actual refunds; any valid refund still has to be honoured. Processor fees may be calculated on tax-inclusive charges and may not be refunded by the provider; the illustrative 3% simplifies this until actual merchant terms are known.

Formula: fixed cohort costs = ₹37,500 (15 founder hours + software + administration). Per learner variable cost = ₹800 acquisition + ₹150 resources + ₹1,333.33 founder feedback + 8% of fee (5% refunds + 3% payments). Contribution per learner = fee minus variable cost. Cohort contribution = learner count × unit contribution − fixed cohort costs. Use full precision before rounding.

At the central price, increasing refund allowance from 5% to 10% reduces the 12-person contribution by ₹4,800 to ₹18,620. Increasing acquisition to ₹1,500 per learner reduces it by ₹8,400 to ₹15,020. Raising founder opportunity cost to ₹3,000/hour reduces it by ₹23,000 to ₹420. At an 18% **illustrative, not determined** GST rate, a tax-inclusive public ₹8,000 fee leaves ₹6,779.66 net before other adjustments; rebuild the model rather than treating tax as margin. The CA must establish the actual tax treatment.
