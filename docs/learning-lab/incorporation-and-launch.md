## India privacy review

The final DPDP Rules notification is dated 13 November 2025; MeitY's document index lists it on 14 November. The Rules stage commencement: rules 1, 2 and 17–21 at publication; rule 4 after one year; rules 3, 5–16, 22 and 23 after 18 months. The Act's commencement notification also stages core processing/rights provisions at 18 months. On 8 October 2026, that later phase has not elapsed; do not use the 2025 draft explanatory note or describe every duty as already commenced. Sources: [final Rules, G.S.R. 846(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf), [Act commencement, G.S.R. 843(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf), [December corrigendum](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/12/3c7ebbae0e5456f493f486e6845df86b.pdf).

Counsel should confirm applicable commencement dates, existing IT/privacy obligations, lawful purposes, processor contracts, notice/consent and withdrawal, security and incident procedures. Build for clear notice, minimal collection, limited access and deletion now; phased commencement is not a reason to collect without care. Adults-only self-confirmation avoids collecting a date of birth by default; it is not rigorous identity proof. Do not collect minors' information or claim a child-data exemption.

## Before any paid service

1. Founder signs off the named programme, dates, capacity, fees/tax display, workload, support, access duration, rubric, completion and reassessment rules. Provide all material terms before purchase.
2. Lawyer confirms current online-service and consumer obligations for the actual operator and the chosen selling model; approves operator/grievance disclosures, fair cancellation/refunds and complaint handling.
3. CA determines service classification, GST registration/treatment, place of supply, invoicing/receipts, tax-inclusive display, refund/credit-note handling, books and income-tax treatment for the founder's actual facts. Do not call the course GST-exempt merely because it is educational.
4. Payment provider confirms eligibility/KYC for the actual unincorporated operator, education service, settlement bank and merchant terms. Eligibility is not assumed and is not a substitute for legal review.
5. Only after those approvals, implement/configure hosted checkout using live secrets outside source control, signed webhook verification, event idempotency, amount/currency/order checks, reconciliation and tested refund handling. Never store cards. An unverified redirect is not proof of payment. Keep payments disabled until all prerequisites and credentials are satisfied.

**Consumer law source caution:** the original 2020 E-Commerce Rules include broad service coverage and operator/grievance disclosures. Rule 4(1)'s original company-form wording was replaced by the 17 May 2021 amendment with conditional wording about a resident nodal officer for the listed entity types. Therefore the original wording must not be used to declare either mandatory immediate incorporation or approval for a sole-proprietor selling model. Obtain advice on the current rules and facts. Sources: [official 2020 Rules](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/E%20commerce%20rules_0.pdf), [official 2021 amendment](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/sites/default/files/Consumer%20Protection%20%28E-Commerce%29%20%28Amendment%29%20Rules%2C%202021.pdf), [Consumer Affairs rules index](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/acts-and-rules/consumer-protection/consumer-protection).

**GST review starting points:** CGST Act section 22 describes turnover-based registration, while section 24 and relevant notifications can affect compulsory registration. Aggregate turnover is not just this website's collections. The education exemption is defined, not universal; CBIC's clarification connects relevant qualification-based exemption to a curriculum for a qualification recognised by law. The Lab's proposed completion certificate does not establish that condition. These starting points are not a determination of the Lab's rate, exemption or registration threshold. Sources: [CBIC CGST Act](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/CGST-bill-e.html) and [CBIC Circular 117/36/2019](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/circular-cgst-117.pdf). ## When incorporation becomes operationally useful

Review incorporation when institutional buyers want to contract with a company, recurring cohorts create material liabilities, co-founders/investors or equity incentives are needed, staff and IP need a durable home, or accounts and merchant arrangements become difficult to manage individually. Do not promise limited liability covers every personal commitment. Formation cost, ongoing accounting/audit/filing and governance responsibilities belong in the decision.

A conventional private company ordinarily needs at least two members and two directors; a One Person Company is a different option. MCA provides SPICe+ and linked incorporation filings. A CS should confirm eligibility, name, objects, subscribers/directors, residence rules, DSC/DIN, registered-office proof, capital, beneficial ownership and current forms. These are not completed registrations. Sources: [Companies Act 2013, sections 3 and 149](https://www.mca.gov.in/content/dam/mca/pdf/CompaniesAct2013.pdf), [MCA SPICe+ instruction kit](https://www.mca.gov.in/Ministry/pdf/SPICe%2B_help.pdf), [MCA V3 incorporation FAQs](https://www.mca.gov.in/content/dam/mca/pdf/SPICEplus-and-linked-filings-FAQs-V3-20230122.pdf).

After approval, the CS/CA must set the actual post-incorporation calendar: company bank account, subscriptions/capital evidence, commencement declaration where applicable, auditor/board requirements, books, statutory registers, tax/payroll registrations and periodic filings. Do not insert a company suffix, CIN or registered office into the website until verified. Do not create fictitious second members/directors to fill a template.

## Transfer from founder to company — nothing is automatic

| Workstream | Required review and concrete action |
|---|---|
| Assets and ownership | Inventory domain, code, original curriculum, artwork, brand, software accounts and expenses. Confirm who owns each asset and whether employer or third-party rights restrict transfer. |
| IP | Execute reviewed assignment or licence from the owner to the company, with scope, consideration, territory and warranties. Assess third-party/open-source terms, trade mark/name conflicts, tax and stamp issues. Do not transfer rights the founder does not own. |
| Existing learner/institution contracts | Identify the contracting operator, remaining teaching/refund/support obligations and assignment restrictions. Obtain novation/consents where needed. A new website footer does not change the contract party. |
| Learner/enquiry data | Determine lawful basis and notice/consent requirements for a new operator and changed processors. Notify people clearly; obtain permission where required; retain purpose/access limits and deletion requests. Do not simply export everything into a company's marketing list. |
| Merchant/bank | Obtain a new or approved transferred merchant arrangement for the company and verified settlement bank. Reconcile outstanding charges, refunds and disputes under the original merchant; provider KYC is not portable by default. |
| Financial records | Reconcile opening assets/liabilities, advances, deferred delivery, founder expenses, refunds, tax records and revenue recognition. CA determines transfer taxes, GST registrations/credits where relevant and separate books. |
| People and services | Review staff/contractor agreements, processor contracts, hosting/authentication ownership, permissions and secrets. Rotate access safely, verify backups and minimise privileged roles. |
| Certificates | Preserve original issuer/date and completion evidence. Define who handles corrections/revocation after transfer. Do not retroactively relabel founder-issued certificates as company-issued. |
| Public configuration | Only after effective transfer, update verified operator identity, contact, policies and invoices with an effective date. Preserve the founder's academic pages and separate commercial claims. |

## IPO aspiration — separate feasibility note

A BSE/NSE listing is a long-term aspiration and belongs in private strategy, not public learning promises. There is no one-year commitment or verified Lab trading history. The venture currently lacks incorporated issuer status and demonstrated financial/operating history. A listing study requires a SEBI-registered merchant banker, CS, audited/restated accounts, governance and material legal/IP/data diligence, not a website traffic target.

The latest consolidated **SEBI ICDR Regulations** found in the official regulations index are amended through 21 March 2026. Main-board regulation 6 includes net tangible assets ₹3 crore in each of three preceding full years, average operating profit ₹15 crore with profit each year, and net worth ₹1 crore each year; an alternative book-built route requires at least 75% QIB allocation and specified refund conditions. SME regulation 229 adds exchange eligibility, ₹1 crore operating profits in two of three years and, after conversion from specified unincorporated forms, at least one full company financial year before draft filing. These are selected conditions, not the full eligibility test or approval. [SEBI consolidated ICDR Regulations](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/mar-2026/1774592300989.pdf), chapters II and IX; [official regulations entry](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/regulations/mar-2026/securities-and-exchange-board-of-india-issue-of-capital-and-disclosure-requirements-regulations-2018-last-amended-on-march-21-2026-_100581.html).

**NSE Emerge:** its page updated 4 August 2026 identifies Indian incorporation, post-issue face-value capital no more than ₹25 crore, a three-year issuer/promoter/predecessor track-record route, operating profits ₹1 crore in two of three years, positive net worth and positive FCFE in two of three preceding years. The promoter route has additional same-business experience/shareholding conditions. This exchange wording must be reconciled with the current SEBI text by advisers; not all founder industry experience is automatically an issuer's eligible record. Listing also requires the prescribed prospectus and approval process. [NSE Emerge eligibility](https://www.nseindia.com/static/companies-listing/raising-capital-public-issues-emerge-eligibility-criteria), [NSE requirements/process](https://www.nseindia.com/static/companies-listing/raising-capital-public-issues-emerge-requirements-and-process).

**BSE:** the official SME eligibility attachment to notice 20231124-54 describes capital, net worth, tangible asset, track record and operating-profit conditions, but is a historical November 2023 notice. The current BSE criteria pages and February 2026 migration notice could not be reliably fetched in this review. The old attachment is not a complete current rulebook, particularly after later SEBI amendments. Do not borrow NSE criteria as BSE criteria or confuse SME admission with migration to a main board. A future BSE study must obtain the latest exchange notices and written adviser interpretation before claiming eligibility. [Historical official BSE SME eligibility attachment](https://www.bseindia.com/markets/MarketInfo/DownloadAttach.aspx?attachedId=d6f4c5cb-9999-4970-80e7-53b7e0978294&id=20231124-54).

Recheck all exchange/SEBI conditions at the time of any financing decision. Hitting a financial threshold does not ensure exchange approval, sufficient investor demand or a sensible listing cost. The next practical milestone is evidence from one responsibly delivered pilot.
