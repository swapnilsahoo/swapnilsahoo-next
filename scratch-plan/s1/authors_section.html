The activity system

Porter's claim is that advantage lives in how activities reinforce each other, not in any one of them. Click a node to see what it supports. A rival copying one node gains almost nothing, because the node's value comes from the ones it is tied to.

First-order fit

Each activity is consistent with the strategy. Copyable one at a time.

Second-order fit

Activities reinforce one another. The whole exceeds the sum.

Third-order fit

Effort is optimised across activities. Hardest of all to imitate.

What a strategy statement is, and is not

Collis and Rukstad's dirty little secret: most executives cannot state the objective, scope and advantage of their business in one simple sentence. Everything above strategy in this hierarchy is useful; none of it tells anyone what to refuse.
