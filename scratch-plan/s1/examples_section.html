Ten positions worth knowing

Who refuses what

Every firm below is famous for what it will not do. Open one to see the refusal, the trade-off that protects it, and the reason a rival cannot simply add the same thing on Monday morning. Judge each one yourself before you open it.

The pattern across all ten

None of these firms is defined by being better. Each is defined by a refusal that costs it something real and would cost a rival more. When you cannot name what a firm refuses, you are usually looking at operational effectiveness wearing the word strategy.
