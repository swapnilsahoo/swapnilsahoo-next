Preparation

Readings for Session 1

Read the case last, after the three articles — it is the material you argue with, and the articles are the tools you argue with. All four are obtained through the course pack; none is reproduced on this page.

  1. Case

    Elon Musk at Tesla (2024)

    Harvard Business School. The session’s anchor case.

  2. Article · Q1

    Porter, M. E. (1996). What Is Strategy? Harvard Business Review.

    Operational effectiveness versus positioning; trade-offs; fit.

  3. Article · Q3

    Mintzberg, H. (1987). Crafting Strategy. Harvard Business Review.

    Deliberate and emergent strategy; strategy as craft rather than plan.

  4. Article · Q2

    Collis, D. J., & Rukstad, M. G. (2008). Can You Say What Your Strategy Is? Harvard Business Review.

    The strategy statement: objective, scope, advantage.
