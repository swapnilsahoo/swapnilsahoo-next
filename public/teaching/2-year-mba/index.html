Strategic Management

A rigorous, case-heavy course on how organisations build and sustain competitive advantage — moving across 20 sessions from internal and external analysis through competitive positioning, corporate strategy, mergers & acquisitions and global strategy. Classical frameworks (Porter, Mintzberg, the resource-based view) are paired with genuinely current cases: Tesla, Nvidia, Amazon, Patagonia and the Tata Group. The course closes with a live field project — a primary interview with a real startup founder or company leader, not a secondary-data write-up.
