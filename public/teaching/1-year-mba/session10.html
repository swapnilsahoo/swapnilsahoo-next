Carried forward · Session 9

A scope decision still needs a route

Session 9 asked which businesses and value-chain stages belong inside the firm. Today asks how to obtain a missing capability. A promising market does not tell you whether to build, license, partner, form a joint venture or acquire. Each route changes who controls the work, who receives the upside and who bears the risk.

Portfolio logic

Capital can move toward attractive opportunities. But owning a portfolio does not automatically create an operating advantage: investors can often diversify for themselves. What does the parent contribute?

Synergy logic

Two activities may be more valuable together through shared assets, lower coordination costs or better customer access. Identify the specific link. “Cross-selling” is a hypothesis until a buyer actually purchases.

Core-competence logic

A repeatable skill may open a new market. Distinguish a transferable routine from a slogan: which people, systems and know-how must move, and what cannot travel?

Transaction-cost logic

Contracts require search, negotiation, monitoring and enforcement. Ownership may reduce bargaining problems when assets are specialised, but it adds bureaucracy, integration costs and the risk of destroying initiative.

Two required readings

Choose the relationship before you choose the target

What has to be combined?

The reading treats alliances and acquisitions as alternatives. Its comparison considers the desired resources and synergies, market conditions and the firm’s ability to collaborate. A familiar deal type can become a habit that prevents a serious comparison.

Reading task: identify a condition favouring an alliance and one favouring acquisition. Then ask whether Amazon needs ownership of the whole organisation or reliable access to a specific capability.

Publisher’s reading record

Are you buying resources or a different growth engine?

The authors distinguish deals that improve the existing business from deals that change its growth trajectory. Absorbing resources can serve the first purpose; preserving a target’s business model may matter for the second. Target selection, price and integration should follow that purpose.

Reading task: name something that integration would improve and something it could destroy. Explain why the same integration plan should not be applied to every acquisition.

The strategic alternatives

Build, borrow or buy is the start of the argument

RouteWhat you gainWhat you must manage
Build internallyControl over development and ownership of learningTime to competence; hiring and experimentation; whether existing routines can produce the capability
Contract or licenseAccess to a defined input without owning the supplierQuality verification, renewal, intellectual-property boundaries and switching costs
Strategic allianceComplementary assets and shared learning while partners remain separateJoint priorities, knowledge leakage, unequal effort and disputes over who captures value
Equity alliance / joint ventureA stronger commitment; a jointly governed vehicle in a JVBoard rights, additional funding, deadlock, performance measures and exit provisions
AcquireOwnership and authority over the acquired assetsPrice, approvals, cultural disruption, retention and execution of the integration plan
Trade-off: control is valuable only if you can use it. Buying an innovative team and removing its decision rights may damage the capability you paid for. Conversely, an alliance may fail when daily decisions require coordination that the contract cannot specify.

Case evidence · Amazon in 2024

One company, several ways to obtain capability

Use the case as a set of observed choices, not proof that every choice created value. The following facts are from pages 1–3 of the assigned case; the questions are our analysis.

Do not mistake the target for the thesis

“Buy Whole Foods” names an action. A thesis says which customer problem becomes easier to solve, which activities produce the improvement and what rivals cannot reproduce easily.

Write the counterfactual

A good acquisition memo compares buying with licensing, partnering, internal development and doing nothing. Without those alternatives, almost any synergy story can sound compelling.

Synthesis

Bring a recommendation that can be challenged

A one-minute investment committee memo

Choose one Amazon move. State the capability gap, compare two routes, describe the value mechanism, identify the integration boundary and name one fact that could overturn your recommendation.

The first 100 days

Which person owns each promised benefit? What will customers and employees notice? Choose one early indicator of capability loss. A falling cost base is not reassuring if the reason customers chose the target is disappearing.

Test your reasoning before class

Can you explain why a contract is insufficient? Have you separated target quality from deal price? Does your integration plan preserve the source of value? If your claim is “culture fit”, name the actual decision, routine or incentive that conflicts.
